DOCUMENT: Getting support for stress in the Netherlands
TOPICS: student support, mentor, study adviser, student psychologist, GP, huisarts

A student does not need to wait until a situation becomes a crisis before asking for help. Support can be useful when stress is persistent, difficult to manage alone, or interfering with study, sleep, relationships, or daily activities.

Possible first contacts:
- Trusted friend, family member, or classmate: emotional support and help thinking through options.
- Tutor, mentor, study adviser, or student counsellor: study planning, academic pressure, workload, extensions, or educational arrangements.
- Student psychologist or student wellbeing service: support for stress, anxiety, low mood, motivation, or personal difficulties. Availability and eligibility vary by institution.
- GP (huisarts): assessment of persistent or severe psychological or physical symptoms and discussion of appropriate care or referral.
- Social worker or municipal support service: practical problems such as finances, housing, debt, or administrative difficulties.

Preparing for an appointment: write down what has been happening; note when it started and whether it is improving or worsening; describe effects on studying, sleep, relationships, and daily activities; list what has already been tried; and write down questions and what kind of help is wanted.

If contacting someone feels difficult, suggest a short message: "I have been struggling with stress and it is affecting my studies. Could we arrange a time to talk about what support is available?"

Services, names, costs, waiting times, and eligibility differ between institutions and municipalities. Milo must not invent institution-specific contact details. Ask which institution or city the student attends before giving local service information, and verify details on official pages. If symptoms are severe, persistent, worsening, or make daily functioning difficult, encourage contact with a GP. Do not label the student as having burnout or another condition.
