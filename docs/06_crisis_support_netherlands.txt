DOCUMENT: Crisis support and immediate safety in the Netherlands
TOPICS: crisis, suicidal thoughts, self-harm, immediate danger, 112, 113
PRIORITY: SAFETY-CRITICAL

This document supports safe routing. It is not a clinical risk assessment or a substitute for emergency services.

IMMEDIATE DANGER
If the user or another person is in immediate physical danger, has made a suicide attempt, or may imminently seriously harm themselves or someone else:
- Tell them to call 112 immediately.
- If possible, encourage them to move to a safer place and involve a trusted person who can stay with them while emergency help is contacted.
- Do not delay emergency action to complete a chatbot conversation, questionnaire, grounding exercise, or web search.
- Do not promise confidentiality or claim Milo can dispatch emergency services.

SUICIDAL THOUGHTS WITHOUT IMMEDIATE LIFE-THREATENING DANGER
In the Netherlands, 113 Zelfmoordpreventie offers free, anonymous support by telephone and chat, day and night:
- Call 113.
- Visit https://www.113.nl/ to start a chat.
Official 113 guidance says to call 112 when someone is in life-threatening danger. 113 is an additional support route when there is no immediate life-threatening danger.

HOW MILO SHOULD RESPOND
- Take statements about suicide or self-harm seriously. Do not dismiss, debate, shame, or moralise.
- Use clear, calm, direct language.
- If danger is unclear, ask a brief direct safety question, such as whether the person is in immediate danger right now. Do not conduct a lengthy risk assessment.
- Encourage contacting a real person now: a trusted person, GP, out-of-hours GP service (huisartsenpost), or 113, depending on urgency.
- If the person says they are in immediate danger, prioritise 112.
- Do not provide methods, instructions, or details that could facilitate self-harm.
- Do not rely on generic relaxation advice as the main response to a crisis.
- Do not claim the chatbot can monitor the user, keep them safe, contact emergency services, or guarantee an outcome.

If a user is worried that another person may be suicidal, call 112 if that person is in immediate life-threatening danger. Otherwise, 113 can also advise people who are worried about someone else.

This document is specific to the Netherlands. If the user is outside the Netherlands, do not assume Dutch numbers are appropriate; ask their location if safe and direct them to local emergency services. Verify emergency numbers and service availability periodically.
